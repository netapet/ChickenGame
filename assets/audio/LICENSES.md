# Audio licenses

## Goat bleats

The goat audio was derived from `goat.aif` by beskhu on Freesound:

https://freesound.org/people/beskhu/sounds/273911/

The source is dedicated under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0). Attribution is
not required. `goat-original.wav` is a direct, unprocessed WAV conversion of
the source. `goat-jump-1.wav` through `goat-jump-4.wav` are unprocessed cuts
from 0.40–1.65, 3.20–4.00, 5.70–6.60, and 8.20–9.20 seconds. The game selects
them randomly for jumping.

https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
